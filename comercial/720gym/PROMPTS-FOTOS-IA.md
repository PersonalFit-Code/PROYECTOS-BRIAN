# 720 GYM CLUB · Prompts de fotos con IA (Nano Banana / Flow)

Para la maqueta del lunes. **Importante:** estas imágenes son para enseñar cómo quedaría la web
completa. Para publicar, lo correcto son fotos reales de 720 (o dejar claro que son ilustrativas):
un cliente que ve unos vestuarios generados y luego encuentra otros distintos se siente engañado.

## Cómo usarlos

1. **Sube siempre como referencia una foto real de la sala** (por ejemplo `720gym/public/fotos/sala-general-grande.webp`)
   y pide que mantenga su estilo. Así todas las imágenes parecen del mismo gimnasio:
   paredes blancas, techo blanco con paneles LED cuadrados, suelo de caucho negro, máquinas y
   rack con estructura negra y tapicería/detalles **rojos**, discos de colores.
2. Formato: **16:9 horizontal, la máxima resolución que dé (mínimo 1920 × 1080)**. Para móvil,
   repite las principales en **9:16**.
3. Nada de logos de otras marcas, texto en las paredes ni marcas de agua.
4. Gente: adultos reales de 25 a 60 años, ropa deportiva lisa (negro, gris, blanco), sin logos.

Frase base que conviene añadir al final de cada prompt:

> Keep the exact interior style of the reference photo: white walls and ceiling with square LED
> panels, black rubber floor, black steel equipment with red upholstery and red accents. Natural,
> realistic photography, shot on a full-frame camera, 24–35 mm lens, soft daylight mixed with
> the LED panels, true-to-life colours, high detail, no text, no logos, no watermark, 16:9.

## 1. Hero de la portada (la más importante)

> Wide establishing shot of a modern, spacious private gym in the morning, empty and spotless,
> camera at chest height looking down the centre of the room towards the back wall, power racks
> and red-and-black machines on both sides, a barbell resting on a red lifting platform in the
> foreground, perfect symmetry, calm and premium atmosphere.

Variante con gente (para «Por qué 720»):

> Same gym, early evening, three people training calmly and well spaced (a woman doing a barbell
> squat in a rack, a man on a red cable machine, a coach giving a tip), the room is far from
> crowded, warm and focused mood.

## 2. Vestuarios, duchas y aseos

> Clean modern gym changing room, matte black lockers with wooden benches, white walls, black
> rubber floor transitioning to grey stone tiles, soft indirect lighting, towels folded on the
> bench, spotless and minimal, nobody inside.

> Modern gym shower area, row of walk-in showers with black matte fixtures, grey large-format
> tiles, glass partitions, warm light, spotless, minimal, nobody inside.

> Minimal gym restroom, black matte taps, grey stone countertop with two basins, round mirror,
> white walls, warm indirect light, spotless.

## 3. Fachada / entrada

> Street-level entrance of a small premium gym in a Spanish city (Ourense, Galicia), glass door
> with black frame, a simple illuminated sign reading "720 GYM" above the door, granite pavement,
> overcast soft light, evening blue hour, inviting.

*(Si la IA escribe mal el rótulo, genera sin texto y ponemos el logo encima.)*

## 4. 720 Pilates (paleta crema, distinta del resto)

> Bright boutique Pilates studio with six Reformer machines in a row, warm cream and sand tones,
> light wooden floor, large window with sheer curtains, soft morning light, plants, a female
> instructor adjusting the posture of a woman on a Reformer, serene and elegant.

> Close-up of hands on the wooden frame and springs of a Pilates Reformer, cream tones, shallow
> depth of field, soft light.

## 5. 720 Yoga

> Calm yoga class in a softly lit room, five people on black mats in a low lunge, instructor at the
> front, warm dim light, minimal decoration, peaceful and grounded.

## 6. CrossGym 720

> Energetic small-group functional training class in the gym, people doing kettlebell swings and
> box jumps on the black rubber floor near the red rig, ropes and wall balls, motion blur on the
> kettlebells, sweat and effort, coach counting reps.

## 7. 720 Powerlifting

> Powerlifter performing a heavy deadlift on a red platform, chalk in the air, coloured competition
> bumper plates, black power rack behind, dramatic side light, low camera angle, intense focus.

> Detail: chalked hands gripping a knurled barbell, coloured plates out of focus, dramatic light.

## 8. Entrenamiento personal

> Personal trainer coaching a client one-to-one in the gym, trainer holding a tablet and pointing
> at the client's hip position during a goblet squat, friendly and professional, natural light.

## 9. Detalles para fondos (texturas)

> Macro shot of stacked coloured bumper plates (red, blue, yellow, green) on a black rack,
> shallow depth of field, moody light.

> Close-up of black rubber gym floor texture with a bit of chalk dust, top-down, soft light.

## 10. Comunidad

> Group of six gym members of different ages laughing together after a session, sitting on a
> bench in the gym, towels and water bottles, natural candid moment, warm light.
